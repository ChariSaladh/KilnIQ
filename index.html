See every tonne and every truck, from quarry to customer.

KilnIQ is the operations system for lime plants. It tracks production, site activity, transport, and profit in one place, so managers stop chasing paper logs.

Request a demo See the dashboard

One record for everything that happens on site

Every entry is made once, where the work happens. Production, transport, and finance read from the same data.

Production

Log limestone received, kiln batches, quicklime and hydrated lime output, and live stock levels.

Trucks and weighbridge

Check vehicles in and out, record gross and tare weights, drivers, destinations, and delivery status.

Site activity

Keep shift logs, maintenance jobs, fuel and coal use, and safety incidents in a searchable timeline.

Profit and income

Filter sales and costs by product, customer, truck, or date, and click through any chart to the records behind it.
